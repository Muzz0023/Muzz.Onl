Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2026

Muzz ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and website (muzz.onl).

Information We Collect

When you use Muzz, we collect:

  • Account Information: Email address and password when you create an account
  • Financial Data: Budget information, assets, investments, bills, and other financial data you choose to enter
  • Usage Data: How you interact with the app to improve our services

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and maintain the Muzz app services
  • Store and sync your financial data across devices
  • Process subscriptions and payments
  • Send important updates about your account
  • Improve and develop new features

Data Storage & Security

Your data is securely stored using Supabase, a trusted cloud database provider. We use industry-standard encryption and security measures to protect your information. Your password is hashed and never stored in plain text.

Data Sharing

We do NOT sell, trade, or share your personal or financial data with third parties. Your financial information stays private and is only accessible to you.

We use the following third-party services:

  • Supabase: Secure database storage and authentication
  • Stripe: Payment processing for subscriptions (we never see your full card details)
  • Google Gemini: AI chat features (conversations are not stored by us)

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your data at any time through the app
  • Export your data using the backup feature
  • Delete your account and all associated data
  • Update or correct your information

Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your data will be permanently removed from our servers.

Children's Privacy

Muzz is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page.
