🦘 Muzz — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Muzz (muzz.onl and the Muzz iOS app) is made and run by Lauchlan, an individual developer based in Queensland, Australia. In this policy, "we" and "us" means Lauchlan.

The short version: Muzz only keeps what you choose to type in, plus anything you choose to bring in from Apple Health. We never connect to your bank, super or share accounts, we don't track your location, we don't show ads, and we never sell or share your data.

1. Information We Collect

Your account

  • Your email address and password, used to sign in. Your password is stored encrypted by our sign-in provider and we can never see it.

What you enter into Muzz

Muzz stores the information you choose to add, so it can show it back to you and keep it in sync across your devices. Depending on which parts of the app you use, this can include:

  • Tasks and reminders — including dates, pinned and renewal/expiry reminders, and a history of the tasks and reminders you've ticked off.
  • Birthdays and dates — names, birthdays, anniversaries and other dates for the people (and pets) you add, plus any photos, gift ideas, notes and gift history you add for them.
  • Timetable — the blocks you add and the hours you choose to show.
  • Gym and health — the workouts you write for each day (such as muscles, exercises, sets, reps, weights and rest days), your step counts, and any body weight readings you type in. Steps are only kept for this week and last week.
  • Bills and income — the bills, amounts, due dates and income you enter, and a monthly history of your bill and income totals.
  • Assets and goals — the assets you add (such as names, categories, values and the date you bought them), a monthly history of their values and your net worth, and your goals, including when they were set and reached.
  • Investments — the holdings and watchlist you enter (such as tickers, number of shares and costs) and any research notes.
  • Settings — such as your chosen colour theme.

Contacts and photos

  • Contacts: if you tap "Import from Contacts", Muzz asks for permission to read your contacts. Only the name, birthday and photo of the contacts that have a birthday are copied into your Birthdays. The rest of your address book is not stored or sent anywhere.
  • Photos and camera: if you add a photo to a birthday, Muzz asks for access to your photo library or camera. Only the photo you pick is used. It is resized to a small image and saved with that birthday.

Apple Health

If you tap "Link" in Gym, Muzz asks for permission to read three things from Apple Health: your steps, your workouts (the date, type and length of each workout) and your body weight. You choose which of these to allow, and you can change or turn off access at any time in the iPhone Settings app (Health → Data Access & Devices → Muzz).

  • Muzz only reads from Apple Health. It never writes to, changes or deletes anything in Apple Health.
  • It reads nothing else, such as heart rate, sleep, medical records or any other health data.
  • What it reads is saved to your Muzz account so it shows in Gym and on your other devices: daily step totals for this week and last week, a daily total of workout minutes and workout types, and body weight readings.
  • Your Apple Health data is used only to show it back to you in Muzz. It is never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, and never shared with anyone, other than being stored with our database provider (Supabase) like the rest of your Muzz data.
  • Health data is not stored in iCloud by Muzz.

Feedback

If you send us feedback or a support request through the app, we receive your message and your email address so we can reply.

What we do not collect

  • Bank, super and share accounts: Muzz never connects to your bank, superannuation or share trading accounts, and never asks for those logins. Any figures in Muzz are ones you typed in yourself.
  • Location: Muzz does not collect your location.
  • Other health data: apart from steps, workouts and body weight you allow from Apple Health, Muzz does not read any health or medical information.
  • Advertising and tracking: Muzz has no ads and does not track you across other apps or websites.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used only to run Muzz for you: to sign you in, to save your data and sync it across your devices, and to show you features such as countdowns, charts, history and goal progress. We use your email to manage your account and to reply if you contact us. We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell it. Data from Apple Health is used only for the Gym features described above.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using Supabase (hosted on Amazon Web Services) with encryption at rest and in transit. Your data is never sold, shared, or provided to third parties, other than the service providers listed below that we need to run Muzz.

Some small settings, such as which walkthroughs you've already seen, are saved only on your device.

If you use "Export data" or share a PDF, the file is created on your device and goes wherever you choose to save or send it (for example iCloud Drive or AirDrop). We do not receive a copy.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Supabase — Database and sign-in
  • Vercel — Website and app server hosting
  • Yahoo Finance — Stock prices, charts and company data
  • ExchangeRate-API — Currency exchange rates
  • Apple — App Store distribution, and iPhone features you choose to use such as Apple Health, Contacts, Photos and the share sheet
  • RevenueCat — Purchase management, included for possible future paid features. Muzz is currently free.

Market data and exchange rates are fetched by our servers using only stock tickers and currency codes. None of your personal information is sent to these providers.

Like most online services, our hosting and database providers automatically keep basic technical logs (such as IP addresses and the times of requests) to keep the service secure and working.

5. How Long We Keep Your Data

We keep your data for as long as you have a Muzz account, so your history (such as net worth, bills, income, gym, workout minutes, body weight and completed tasks) keeps building over time. Step counts are only kept for this week and last week. If you turn off Apple Health access, Muzz stops reading new data, and anything already saved stays in your account until you delete it or delete your account. You can delete individual items at any time, or delete your whole account as described below.

6. Account Deletion

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from the Account section on the app's dashboard. This action is permanent and cannot be undone.

7. Other People's Information

Some features let you store details about other people, such as names, birthdays and photos of family and friends. Please only add information you're comfortable keeping, and that the people involved would reasonably expect you to have. We only use this information to show it back to you.

8. Children's Privacy

Muzz is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

9. Your Rights

You can view, change and export your data in the app at any time, and you can delete your account. If you'd like help accessing or correcting your information, or have a privacy concern, contact us using the details below. You can also contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC).

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

11. Contact
