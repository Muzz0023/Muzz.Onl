🦘 Muzz — Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 8, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Muzz collects the following information when you create an account:

  • Email address (for authentication)
  • Data you enter into the app (finances, tasks, goals, habits, etc.)

We do not collect your name, phone number, location, or any other personal information unless you voluntarily enter it into the app.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely to provide and improve the Muzz app experience. We use your email to authenticate your account and your app data to sync across devices via our secure database.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using Supabase (hosted on Amazon Web Services) with encryption at rest and in transit. Your data is never sold, shared, or provided to third parties.

4. AI Chat

Muzz includes an AI assistant powered by Google Gemini. When you use the chat feature, your messages are sent to Google's API to generate responses. We do not store chat history on our servers beyond your current session.

5. Third-Party Services

  • Supabase — Database and authentication
  • Google Gemini — AI chat functionality
  • RevenueCat — Subscription management (iOS)
  • Stripe — Payment processing (web)
  • Finnhub — Stock price data

6. Account Deletion

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from the app's sidebar menu. This action is permanent and cannot be undone.

7. Children's Privacy

Muzz is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at Muzz.onl@outlook.com

Data Storage & Security

Your data is securely stored using Supabase, a trusted cloud database provider. We use industry-standard encryption and security measures to protect your information. Your password is hashed and never stored in plain text.

Data Sharing

We do NOT sell, trade, or share your personal or financial data with third parties. Your financial information stays private and is only accessible to you.

We use the following third-party services:

  • Supabase: Secure database storage and authentication
  • Stripe: Payment processing for subscriptions (we never see your full card details)
  • Google Gemini: AI chat features (conversations are not stored by us)

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Export your data using the backup feature
  • Delete your account and all associated data
  • Update or correct your information

Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your data will be permanently removed from our servers.
